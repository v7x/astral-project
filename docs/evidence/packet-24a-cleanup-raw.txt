The ambiguous case injects a successful fusermount return while `ismount` remains true. The failed-unmount case injects a nonzero fusermount result. The uncertain case injects a queue failure. All three preserve a live-content marker under the live/uncertain mount path, remain `DRAINING`, and avoid recursive traversal or deletion.
